Browser install

Adds Just Canvass to your home screen so it opens in one tap. Optional — the browser version is the same app. Use Chrome on Android or Safari on iPhone; other browsers can't install it.

Android

Chrome

  1. Open app.justcanvass.ca in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots at the top right.
  3. Tap Install (or Add to Home screen) and confirm.
iPhone & iPad

Safari, iOS 18 and newer

  1. Open app.justcanvass.ca in Safari.
  2. Tap the three dots, then Share.
  3. Tap More, then Add to Home Screen.
  4. Turn on Open as Web App if you see it, then tap Add.
Older iPhones

Safari, iOS 12 to 17

  1. Open app.justcanvass.ca in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom — the square with an arrow.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Questions

Is it on the App Store?

Coming soon. Until then the Safari home-screen install gives you the same app with the same icon.

Do I have to install it?

No. Open app.justcanvass.ca and you're in. Installing only puts the icon on your home screen.

Do volunteers need their own account?

Yes. Accounts are invite-only — your campaign sends invites from the Users screen. Install first if you like; sign in once the invite arrives.

Does it cost volunteers anything?

No. Campaigns pay one flat fee that includes unlimited users.

Why can't I find the install option?

Wrong browser, almost always. iPhone needs Safari; Android needs Chrome. Still nothing? Check your home screen — it may already be installed.
