2026 Canadian Municipal Election Dates & Deadlines: A Candidate's Calendar

Updated July 2026 · Ontario, British Columbia & Manitoba

If you're running for mayor, council, or school board trustee in 2026, your whole campaign is built around a handful of fixed dates — the day nominations open, the deadline to file, and election day itself. In the three provinces Just Canvass covers most closely — Ontario, British Columbia, and Manitoba — voters all head to the polls within about two weeks of each other in October 2026.

Here's the candidate's calendar for each province, with the nomination windows, fees, and deadlines you need. Every date below is drawn from official election sources, linked in each section.

Confirm before you file. Dates and rules can change, and exact local deadlines — especially outside the big cities — are set by your municipal clerk or Senior Election Official. Always confirm with your municipality, Elections BC, or Elections Manitoba before you act. Last verified July 2026.

Ontario: Monday, October 26, 2026

Ontario runs its municipal elections on a fixed four-year cycle, on the fourth Monday of October. In 2026 that's Monday, October 26, 2026.

  • Nominations open: May 1, 2026.
  • Nomination Day (filing & withdrawal deadline): Friday, August 21, 2026, at 2:00 p.m.
  • Clerk certification: by 4:00 p.m. on Monday, August 24, 2026.
  • Filing fee: $200 for head of council (mayor); $100 for councillor or school board trustee. The fee is refunded if you file your financial statement on time.
  • Endorsement signatures: in municipalities with 4,000 or more electors, candidates must submit 25 endorsement signatures with their nomination.

Full details are in the province's official 2026 Candidates' Guide, and the Association of Municipalities of Ontario (AMO) keeps a good overview. Your own city or town clerk publishes the local specifics. For a fuller walkthrough, see our 2026 Ontario municipal election candidate guide.

British Columbia: Saturday, October 17, 2026

British Columbia holds general local elections every four years on the third Saturday of October. In 2026 that's Saturday, October 17, 2026. Voters elect mayors, councillors, school trustees, and regional district electoral area directors across the province.

  • Nomination period: September 1 to September 11, 2026 (nominations close at 4:00 p.m. on Friday, September 11).
  • Who runs the vote: each local government administers its own election — voting, counting, and accepting nominations.
  • Campaign finance & advertising: administered province-wide by Elections BC under the Local Elections Campaign Financing Act.

See Elections BC's 2026 General Local Elections page for the campaign-financing rules, and your municipality's website for local nomination details. For a fuller walkthrough, see our 2026 BC municipal election candidate guide.

Manitoba: Wednesday, October 28, 2026

Manitoba holds its general municipal elections on the fourth Wednesday of October — Wednesday, October 28, 2026 — for mayors, councillors, and school trustees across the province, including Winnipeg.

  • Winnipeg nomination period: September 16 to September 22, 2026, with all papers filed in person by 4:30 p.m. on Tuesday, September 22.
  • Registering a campaign (Winnipeg): councillor and school-trustee candidates can register starting June 30, 2026; mayoral candidates must register by September 22, 2026.
  • Elsewhere in Manitoba: other municipalities vote the same day but set nomination handling through their local Senior Election Official — confirm your dates locally.

Winnipeg's official 2026 election site and Elections Manitoba have the authoritative details. For a fuller walkthrough, see our 2026 Manitoba municipal election candidate guide.

A note on Alberta

Alberta isn't on the 2026 general schedule — its last general municipal election was on October 20, 2025, and the next is in 2029. That said, Alberta municipalities still hold by-elections between cycles as seats come open, and Just Canvass works just as well for a by-election, a nomination-contest push, or year-round candidate organizing. Check your municipality's website for any by-election dates.

Your 2026 campaign timeline

Once you map backward from your province's dates, a municipal campaign falls into four phases:

  • Now → spring: build the foundation. Line up your team, sharpen your message, and get to know your district. With Just Canvass, every household in your ward is pre-loaded, so you can plan turf before you file.
  • Nominations open (May in Ontario, later in BC/MB): file and start early. Submit your papers, collect endorsement signatures where required, and begin light door-knocking to build name recognition.
  • Summer → early fall: peak canvassing. This is the ground game — door-to-door, lawn signs, and phone, text, and email outreach to the residents you've met.
  • Final two weeks: get out the vote. Everything you've learned about who supports you turns into turnout. Just Canvass ties door-knocking and phone banking together so a voter reached one way is automatically crossed off for the other — no duplicate contacts on the days that matter most.

Frequently asked questions

When is the 2026 municipal election in Ontario?

Ontario's 2026 municipal election is on Monday, October 26, 2026. Nominations open May 1, 2026 and close on Nomination Day, Friday, August 21, 2026 at 2:00 p.m.

When is the 2026 municipal election in British Columbia?

British Columbia's 2026 general local elections are on Saturday, October 17, 2026. The nomination period runs from September 1 to September 11, 2026.

When is the 2026 municipal election in Manitoba?

Manitoba's 2026 general municipal elections, including Winnipeg, are on Wednesday, October 28, 2026. In Winnipeg the nomination period runs September 16 to September 22, 2026, with a filing deadline of 4:30 p.m. on September 22.

How much does it cost to file nomination papers in Ontario?

In Ontario the nomination filing fee is $200 for head of council (mayor) and $100 for councillor or school board trustee. In municipalities with 4,000 or more electors, candidates must also submit 25 endorsement signatures with their nomination.

When should I start canvassing for a 2026 municipal campaign?

Start as early as you can. Many candidates begin door-knocking in the spring and summer once nominated to build name recognition, then intensify canvassing, phone, text and get-out-the-vote outreach through the fall up to election day in October 2026.
