2026 Manitoba Municipal Election: Dates, Deadlines & a Candidate's Guide

Updated July 2026 · Manitoba

Manitoba's 2026 general municipal elections are Wednesday, October 28, 2026 — the last of the three provinces Just Canvass covers to vote this fall. Across the province, voters elect mayors, councillors, and school trustees, including in the City of Winnipeg. Manitoba's rules are set locally through each municipality's Senior Election Official, and Winnipeg — by far the biggest race — has its own registration windows and signature requirements.

Here's the Manitoba candidate's calendar, focused on Winnipeg where the rules are most specific, plus how to plan your canvassing around it.

Confirm before you file. Election day is province-wide, but nomination handling, registration windows, and signature counts are set locally — and the details below are for the City of Winnipeg. Outside Winnipeg, confirm with your municipality's Senior Election Official and Elections Manitoba. Last verified July 2026.

Election day: Wednesday, October 28, 2026

Manitoba holds its general municipal elections on the fourth Wednesday of October — October 28, 2026 — for mayors, councillors, and school trustees across the province, including Winnipeg. It's the only province of the three we cover that votes midweek.

Registration and nominations in Winnipeg

Winnipeg splits the process into two steps: registering as a candidate (which you must do before raising or spending campaign money) and later filing nomination papers during the formal nomination period.

  • Registration windows: mayoral candidates may register starting May 1, 2026; councillor and school-trustee candidates starting June 30, 2026. All candidates must be registered by the close of nominations.
  • Nomination period: September 16 to September 22, 2026, with all papers filed in person with the Senior Election Official by 4:30 p.m. on Tuesday, September 22.
  • Signatures to run for mayor: a Winnipeg mayoral nomination requires 250 signatures. You can collect signatures ahead of time, but the papers themselves must be filed during the nomination period.
  • Where you file: in person at the City Clerk's Department during nomination-period hours.

Winnipeg's official 2026 election site has the candidate handbook, ward maps, and full nomination process; Elections Manitoba covers province-wide rules.

Outside Winnipeg

Every other Manitoba municipality votes on the same day, October 28, 2026, but sets its own nomination handling through its local Senior Election Official — including nomination hours, any signature requirements, and where to file. If you're running outside Winnipeg, your municipal office is the authoritative source; confirm your dates locally well before September.

Frequently asked questions

When is the 2026 municipal election in Manitoba?

Manitoba's 2026 general municipal elections, including Winnipeg, are on Wednesday, October 28, 2026.

When can candidates register for the 2026 Winnipeg election?

In Winnipeg, mayoral candidates may register from May 1, 2026, and councillor and school-trustee candidates from June 30, 2026. All candidates must be registered and file nomination papers by 4:30 p.m. on September 22, 2026.

How many signatures do you need to run for mayor of Winnipeg?

Running for mayor of Winnipeg requires 250 signatures on your nomination papers. Nomination papers must be filed in person with the Senior Election Official during the nomination period.

What about municipalities outside Winnipeg?

Other Manitoba municipalities vote on the same day, but each sets its own nomination handling through its local Senior Election Official. Confirm your nomination dates and requirements with your municipality.

When should I start canvassing for a 2026 Manitoba campaign?

Start early. Registration opens months before the vote, so build your team, message, and turf plan through spring and summer, then run an intensive door-knocking, phone, text and GOTV push from the September nomination period through to election day.
